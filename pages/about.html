Who we are

Faith that is
lived together.

Saint Mary and Archangel Michael is a parish of the Coptic Orthodox Church. We gather around the Holy Eucharist, learn from the Scriptures and the lives of the saints, and encourage one another to love God and our neighbors.

Our doors are open to people at every stage of their journey. Come as you are, find a place to belong, and grow with us.
